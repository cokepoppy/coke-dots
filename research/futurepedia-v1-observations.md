## Evidence limits

- This is a third-party hands-on demonstration, not an OpenAI product specification. Treat the visible screens as evidence of what this video presents; treat spoken product claims and successful completion messages as claims by the demonstrator until independently verified.
- The player was navigated by chapter and inspected in Chrome at 0:02, 1:18, 2:46, 4:00, 5:07, 5:17, 6:06, 6:26, 7:44, 9:28, 10:01, and 10:11. Full Chrome-window screenshots of 1:18, 2:46, 4:00, 5:07, 6:06, 6:26, and 9:28 are saved locally in `research/frames/` (ignored by Git); the 6:26 player crop is also saved separately. The full-window PNGs are 3164 × 1946 pixels, captured from the visible Chrome window on 2026-10-05. They include browser chrome, YouTube controls, the rendered video, and its watermark; they are not source-video frames. Other sampled times were inspected but are not persisted. The video remains at its source URL.
- The YouTube transcript panel showed a loading spinner. Captions visible in the player and the chapter list were usable; no transcript export was available in this session.
- The browser screenshot is not the decoded video source frame. The stream resolution, browser scaling, and source UI viewport are unknown. No CSS-pixel measurements or pixel-match claim can be made from this review.
- The Futurepedia watermark and presenter overlay obscure parts of some frames. Several chapter entry frames show the presenter rather than the product UI; those gaps are marked below.

## Replication implications

1. The sampled desktop shell has a persistent left icon rail, a separate navigation list, and a broad main conversation area. The top Chat/Work switch appears in the landing view; do not assume it is present in every conversation state without checking more frames.
2. A new dot conversation is introduced by a centered avatar and greeting, followed by an explicit naming question and a scratchpad link. The video does not show changing shape, color, eyes, glasses, or accessories.
3. A user gives one compound goal and Dot describes breaking it across Work, another chat, and Codex. The visible return appears in chat as a project page, thumbnail, and motion-graphics result. Exact task cards, status transitions, and execution timing require closer frame review.
4. The phone call uses a full-height dark interface with avatar, elapsed-time display, and speaker/end/mute controls. This sample is too compressed to measure exact layout or fonts.
5. Scheduled has its own left navigation and a monitoring status. The demonstrated error opening a listed chat is part of the evidence and should be included in failure-state testing.
6. Pixel-level comparison remains pending. The saved 3164 × 1946 images capture the whole Chrome window and a compressed YouTube rendition, so they document visible states but cannot supply product CSS-pixel dimensions. Next capture clean, stable reference images at a known source viewport when available, then measure and compare matching product regions against Coke Dots.
